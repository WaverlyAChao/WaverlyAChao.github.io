Overview

As head of build during sophomore year and team captain during junior and senior years, I managed team meetings, approved designs, handled electrical work, and documented progress.

Our team qualified for championships for the first time in almost ten years. I also created curricula and taught classes of up to 30 third- and fourth-grade girls, becoming a role model for the next generation of women in STEM.
