FIRST Tech Challenge

Team Captain

Throughout my four years of high school, I spent most afternoons working on a wide range of tasks for our robotics team. As head of build during sophomore year and team captain during junior and senior years, I managed team meetings, approved designs, handled electrical work, and documented progress while creating curricula and teaching classes of up to 30 third- and fourth-grade girls. Although the team qualified for championships for the first time in almost ten years, my greatest achievement was becoming a role model for the next generation of women in STEM.
